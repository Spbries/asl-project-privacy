Delete your ASL Project account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the ASL Project app, developed by Steven Briesacher.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the app and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Delete without the app

Email [CONTACT EMAIL – ADD BEFORE PUBLISHING] from the email address on your account and ask us to delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account
  • Your profile and bio
  • Your learning reasons
  • Your streaks and study progress
  • Your favorites
  • Your custom quizzes

Timing and retention

Deletion is completed within 30 days. We keep nothing, except where the law requires us to.
