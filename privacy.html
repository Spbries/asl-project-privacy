Privacy policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Who we are

This policy covers the ASL Project mobile app ("the app"), developed by Steven Briesacher ("we", "us").

Data we collect

  • Account information: your email address and name, from email sign-in or Google sign-in.
  • Profile: your profile details and an optional bio.
  • Learning reasons: the reasons for learning you choose during onboarding (optional).
  • Progress: your streaks and study progress.
  • Favorites: the signs you mark as favorites.
  • Custom quizzes: quizzes you create and save.

How we use your data

We use your data only to run the app and save your progress. We do not show ads, we do not use analytics, and we do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that run the app:

  • Supabase, for hosting, database and authentication.
  • Google, if you choose Google sign-in.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account.

Children

The app is for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. If we learn that a user is under 13, we will delete their account and data.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and data in the app or by email. See how to delete your account.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above.
